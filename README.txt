====================================================================
MUSIC ASSET GUIDE FOR SARA'S PROPOSAL
====================================================================

You have two wonderful audio options for this website:

1. BUILT-IN ROMANTIC PIANO SYNTHESIZER (Active by default!)
   You don't need to download or copy anything! The website contains
   a procedural Web Audio romantic piano/music-box synthesizer that
   plays soft, dreamy anime-romance arpeggios automatically.

2. CUSTOM MP3 SONG
   If you have a favorite romantic song or instrumental for Sara
   (e.g., an acoustic guitar song, piano ballad, or anime theme):
   - Name your file: "romantic-melody.mp3"
   - Place it right here in this folder: "assets/music/romantic-melody.mp3"
   - The website will automatically detect and play your custom MP3!
